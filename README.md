# Privacy Policy for Power Bell

**Effective date:** 3 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with the mobile game **Power Bell** (the "Game"), published by Fanzone Interactive.

Please read this policy before installing or using the Game. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Game.

---

## 1. Summary

**Power Bell does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

The Game works entirely offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking technologies, no third-party SDKs, and no user accounts. Everything the Game saves stays on your own device.

---

## 2. Information we do NOT collect

We want to be explicit, because many mobile games do collect this data and we do not. The Game does **not**:

- collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details;
- create or require a user account;
- collect or use the Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / IDFA);
- collect device identifiers, IP address, or location data;
- contain any advertising SDK or show any advertisements;
- contain any analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting, or attribution SDK;
- track your behaviour across other apps or websites;
- transmit any data to us or to any third party over the internet.

The Game contains no networking code and does not send or receive data from any server. The only links it can open are two buttons on its About screen, and only when you tap them: **Rate** opens the Game's page in Google Play, and **Privacy** opens this policy in your browser. Those pages are governed by Google's and GitHub's own privacy policies.

---

## 3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue playing where you left off, the Game saves progress **locally on your device only**, in the platform's standard application preferences. This information is never uploaded anywhere. It contains:

- the highest level reached, per-level star results and your best score;
- your in-game coin balance;
- which cosmetic items are unlocked and equipped (mallets, belts);
- spare lives owned;
- daily capsule gift date and day streak;
- selected language, sound and vibration settings.

All of the above is gameplay data only. None of it identifies you as a person.

**Deleting this data:** uninstalling the Game removes all of it from your device. On Android you can also use *Settings → Apps → Power Bell → Storage → Clear data*. There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we never receive it.

The Game may use the device's vibration motor for short haptic feedback. You can switch it off in the Game's settings. This does not involve any data.

---

## 4. In-app purchases

The Game displays an in-game shop that uses **virtual currency earned through gameplay only**. The current version processes no real-money transactions and integrates no payment or billing SDK. We therefore receive no payment information of any kind.

If real-money purchases are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any payment processing would be handled by the platform store (Google Play or the Apple App Store) under their own privacy policies — we would still never receive your payment details.

---

## 5. Third-party services

**None.**

The Game integrates no third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect data.

---

## 6. Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience, including children.

Because the Game collects no personal data at all, transmits nothing, contains no advertising and no third-party SDKs, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age. This is consistent with the requirements of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and with Article 8 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information, please contact us at the address in Section 9 — although, as described above, we hold no user data to remove.

---

## 7. Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area / United Kingdom) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) grant you rights over personal data held about you, including the rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability, and the right not to have personal information sold or shared.

**We hold no personal data about you**, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or erase, and we have never sold or shared personal information. All data the Game creates is under your direct control on your own device and can be erased at any time by uninstalling the Game.

Material changes will be reflected in the store listing before the affected version is released. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
